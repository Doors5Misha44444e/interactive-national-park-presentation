01 — Location

Where is it?

Synevyr National Nature Park is located in the heart of the Ukrainian Carpathian Mountains, in the western part of Ukraine.

  • 📍 Oblast: Zakarpattia (Transcarpathia) Region
  • 🏘️ Nearest town: Mizhhiria
  • 🧭 Coordinates: ~48°37′N, 23°41′E
  • ⛰️ Altitude: 700–1,700 m above sea level
  • 🌍 Borders: Carpathian Biosphere Reserve

The park lies within the picturesque Gorgany and Polonyna Borzhava mountain ranges, making it a strategic ecological corridor in Central Europe.

02 — Landscape

What does the landscape look like?

Synevyr is a stunning mosaic of mountains, alpine lakes, dense forests, and rushing rivers.

🏔️

Mountains

Carpathian peaks reaching up to 1,700 m, including mount Kamianka and Stohy.

💎

Alpine Lakes

The famous Lake Synevyr — "the Sea of the Carpathians" — sits at 989 m above sea level.

🌲

Forests

Beech, fir, and spruce forests cover most of the park's territory.

💧

Rivers

Crystal-clear mountain streams such as the Tysa and Chorna rivers.

🪨

Glacial Terrain

Steep slopes, rocky outcrops and glacial valleys shaped during the Ice Age.

🌄

Meadows

Subalpine polonyny (highland meadows) carpet the mountaintops.

03 — Biodiversity

Animals and Plants

Home to rare, endangered and protected species of Ukrainian wildlife.

🌿

Flora

  • European Beech (Fagus sylvatica) — dominant species
  • Norway Spruce and Silver Fir
  • Edelweiss — rare alpine flower
  • Carpathian bluebell & orchids
  • Rhododendron of the Carpathians
  • Rare: Lady's slipper orchid (Cypripedium calceolus)
🦌

Fauna

  • Brown Bear 🐻 — protected population
  • Wolf and European Lynx
  • Red Deer, Roe Deer, Wild Boar
  • Western Capercaillie (rare grouse)
  • Golden Eagle & Black Stork
  • Protected: Carpathian newt and trout
04 — Climate

What is the climate like?

A cool mountain climate with four distinct seasons, shaped by altitude.

🌸

Spring

+5°C to +15°C

Snow melts, rivers swell, wildflowers bloom across the meadows.

☀️

Summer

+15°C to +25°C

Warm and humid; perfect hiking season with green forests and clear lakes.

🍂

Autumn

+3°C to +12°C

Forests turn gold and crimson; misty mornings over Lake Synevyr.

❄️

−5°C to −15°C

05 — Conservation

Why is it a National Park?

Established on 5th September 1989, Synevyr protects one of the most valuable natural ecosystems in Ukraine.

1

Ecological Importance

Protects the highest-altitude lake in the Carpathians (Lake Synevyr) — a natural monument of national significance.

2

Biodiversity Sanctuary

Shelters more than 60 species listed in the Red Book of Ukraine.

3

Watershed Protection

Safeguards the headwaters of the Tysa River, vital for the region's freshwater supply.

4

Research & Education

Serves as an open-air laboratory for scientists and a learning site for school geography projects like this one.

5

Ecotourism

Promotes sustainable tourism that respects nature and supports local Hutsul communities.
